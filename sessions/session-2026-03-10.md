## What I understood
Set operations combine results from multiple queries.

- UNION merges results and removes duplicates.
- UNION ALL merges results and keeps duplicates.
- MINUS returns rows from the first query that are not in the second.
- INTERSECTION returns only rows that appear in both queries.
